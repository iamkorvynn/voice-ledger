# VoiceLedger — Play Store listing

## Short description (80 chars max)

Speak an expense, confirm it, track it. Voice expense tracker, on-device.

## Long description

VoiceLedger is the fastest way to track what you spend. No forms, no fuss —
just say it.

Tap the big mic and say "spent 200 on lunch" or "paid 500 for petrol".
VoiceLedger understands the amount and the category, shows you what it heard,
and saves it only after you confirm. Your money, your words, your call.

Everything is stored on your phone in a local database. There is no account,
no cloud sync and no sample data — the app starts empty and fills up with
your real spending.

What you get:

- Voice expense entry with on-device speech recognition
- Smart on-device parsing that works offline — amounts, categories, notes
- Optional Gemini-powered parsing with your own API key (stored only on
  your device)
- Monthly totals, per-category pie chart and full expense history
- Add, edit and delete expenses manually
- Export any month — or all your expenses — as CSV
- First-run onboarding that explains the whole flow in three steps

Perfect for anyone who hates typing expenses into a spreadsheet but still
wants to know where the money went.
